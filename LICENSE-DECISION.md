# License decision

The factory repository is public by the owner's decision, but publication does not grant an open-source license. Unless a later explicit legal decision adds a license, the contents are distributed with all rights reserved.

Each product repository receives a separate license decision in its Product Contract. MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, GPL, AGPL or another license must never be selected solely by an agent's preference. Strong-copyleft or unclear dependency licenses are handled by `policies/repository-policy.yaml` and `policies/security-policy.yaml`.
